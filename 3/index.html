Topic Description

When I first transferred to Ole Miss, I wanted to minor in Astronomy. This pushed me into taking Astronomy of stars and Physics. Once I began learning more, I fell in love with space more each day. Eventhough I didn't pursue the minor, I continue to have the love and appreciation for astronomy. I want people to experience space and what it has to offer. Most people only know the basics and settle for the solar system. I want to teach others of the complexity of stars, the variety of galaxies, and expand on the typical solar system people learn. This will show people the vastness atronomy has to offer.

I think the issue with existing websites is the lack of variety. You either go on a website to learn about stars and the constellations, or you learn about the planets in the solar system. For constellations, I find websites such as Stellarium . Its a great site however its heavily interactive which might confuse some users on how to access the information. The same applies to official websites such as Nasa's . While these websites are beautiful, they can be confusing to older users and younger ones.
