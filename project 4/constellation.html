CONSTELLATIONS.

Constellations are not actual objects in the sky. They are made from human connecting the stars and adding myths. Most constellations are named after greek mythology stories. An example would be Orion. Many people can spot his constellation through the stars called Orion's Belt. As of current date there is 88 recognized constellations by NASA. Since constellations are determined by perspective, the stars in a constellation are not close to each other at all.
